01
The Big Trade-Off

Default-on AI
vs. deliberate control.

The Tension

In the next ten weeks Microsoft switches AI capability and spend on by default. Claude models go live for all users in Swiss and EU tenants on 9 December unless an admin opts out. Metered Cowork, Code and Autopilot become visible to every licensed user from the end of January. Usage-based billing turns on by default for new Copilot Business orders in Q1 2027, capped at 4,000 Copilot Credits per user per month. Copilot Chat limits already nudge unlicensed staff to request or buy seats. Accepting the defaults speeds adoption, but spreads a new AI subprocessor, a consumption bill and more non-human identities across the tenant without anyone having decided.

The Leadership Call

Do we let vendor defaults set the pace and monitor after the fact, or opt out across the board and accept that blocked demand moves to shadow AI? DNSFilter reports that 20.1% of organisations have already had a security incident from an unapproved AI connection.

The Decision

Decide every default before its date, with a named owner: enable, scope to groups, or switch off. Scope Claude to specific groups by 9 December, set Copilot spending limits below the 4,000-credit default before the next order, allow access requests for metered experiences by role, and move third-party AI apps to admin consent. Opt in by decision, not by default.

The 4-Pillar Executive Agenda

Four pillars, one decision each.

1
Budget
Financial & Licensing Realities

Seats become seats plus a meter

The Reality Check

From 1 December, Copilot can be bought without a Microsoft 365 base licence, and new SKU IDs replace the '(add-on)' offers for new purchases. In Q1 2027, new Copilot Business orders through CSP switch on pay-as-you-go by default, and one new order also turns metering on for the Copilot Business licences you already have. Analysts meanwhile estimate that about one in three paid Copilot seats goes unused in a typical week.

The Leadership Action

Set tenant spending limits and alerts below the 4,000-credit default before the next Copilot Business order, and ask your CSP for the go-live date. Reclaim seats idle for 28 days or more before renewal, and update quote templates and software asset management rules for the new SKU IDs.

Risk
Governance & Data Hygiene

Every AI path needs an owner

The Reality Check

Three new paths into company data open at once. Claude becomes a Microsoft subprocessor in everyday use on 9 December. Agent identities sit outside Conditional Access policies aimed at 'all users', and idle ones will be disabled after 60 days by a new default policy. And employees grant third-party AI tools OAuth access: 43% of organisations only find out afterwards, or never.

The Leadership Action

Legal and Security record the Anthropic choice before 9 December and update the record of processing activities. Inventory agent identities, give each a human sponsor, and add agent-targeted Conditional Access in report-only mode first. Switch Entra to the admin consent workflow for third-party apps.

DEX
Digital Employee Experience

Patch regressions and sign-in changes hit the helpdesk

The Reality Check

The November Windows updates can fail to print documents with invisible zero-size images (watermarks, generated templates), broke KMS activation scripts until the 13 November out-of-band fix, and October's LDAP hardening can cut access to some NetApp SMB shares. Admins report NTLM signing breaking App Volumes in VDI. Some SMS and voice MFA sign-ins now show a CAPTCHA, and cross-tenant calendars with partners break unless moved off EWS.

The Leadership Action

Keep the security update deployed. Have template owners in Finance, Legal and HR test print-critical documents this week, and start the NTLM signing audit well before May 2027. Prepare helpdesk scripts for the MFA CAPTCHA and Copilot Chat limits, and migrate cross-tenant calendar sharing after 23 November together with partner admins.

4
Culture
Culture, Adoption & Change Velocity

Measure pull, not push

The Reality Check

Licences assigned are not adoption: about a third of paid seats sit idle in a typical week. At the same time, Copilot Chat limit prompts and the new 'Request access' buttons will create a stream of bottom-up demand, and blocking without an approved alternative pushes staff towards unapproved tools.

The Leadership Action

Treat licence and access requests as the demand signal. Route them through one approval path that asks which business process the seat will speed up, point 'Learn more' to internal guidance with champion examples, and publish an HR-owned acceptable-use policy that names the approved tools. Report time saved in named processes, not licences assigned.

04
The Anti-Pattern · What to stop doing this week
Stop letting default settings and upgrade prompts make your AI decisions.

This quarter, Microsoft's defaults decide who gets Claude, who sees metered agents and when consumption billing starts, while Chat limit prompts push staff to ask for seats. Buying seats in response to prompts, or leaving defaults untouched because nobody owns them, produces shelfware and unbudgeted consumption at the same time. Every default on the cliff list below needs a named owner and a recorded choice before its date.
