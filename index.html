01
The Big Trade-Off

Default-on AI
vs. deliberate control.

The Tension

Over the next ten weeks Microsoft switches on AI features and AI spending, unless someone switches them off first. Claude models go live for all users in Swiss and EU tenants on 9 December unless an admin opts out. From the end of January, every licensed user will see metered Cowork, Code and Autopilot. In Q1 2027, new Copilot Business orders come with usage-based billing switched on, capped at 4,000 Copilot Credits per user per month. Copilot Chat limits are already nudging unlicensed staff to request or buy seats. Saying yes to all of this speeds up adoption. It also brings in a new AI subprocessor and a bill that grows with use, and the tenant fills up with more non-human identities. Nobody actually decided any of it.

The Leadership Call

Do we let vendor defaults set the pace and check afterwards? Or do we opt out across the board and accept that blocked demand moves to shadow AI? That second risk isn't theoretical. DNSFilter reports that 20.1% of organisations have already had a security incident from an unapproved AI connection.

The Decision

Decide each setting before its date, and write down who owns it. Scope Claude to specific groups by 9 December. Before the next Copilot order, set spending limits below that credit cap. Let staff request metered experiences by role, and move third-party AI apps to admin consent. Opting in is fine, as long as someone actually chose to.

The Reality Check

From 1 December, Copilot can be bought without a Microsoft 365 base licence, and new SKU IDs replace the '(add-on)' offers for new purchases. The bigger change comes in Q1 2027. New Copilot Business orders through CSP then switch on pay-as-you-go, and a single new order also turns on metering for the Copilot Business licences you already have. Buy one seat, meter them all. Meanwhile, analysts estimate that about one in three paid Copilot seats goes unused in a typical week.

The Leadership Action

Ask your CSP when this goes live for you. Before the next Copilot Business order, set tenant spending limits and alerts below the credit cap from the trade-off above. At renewal, reclaim seats that have been idle for 28 days or more. Quote templates and software asset management rules need the new SKU IDs too, because anything that matches on the old offer names stops matching.

The Reality Check

Several new ways into company data open within a few weeks. On 9 December Claude becomes a Microsoft subprocessor in everyday use. Agent identities aren't covered by Conditional Access policies aimed at 'all users', so in Entra 'all' turns out to mean 'all humans'. A new built-in policy will also disable idle agents after 60 days. And employees keep granting third-party AI tools OAuth access, which 43% of organisations only find out about afterwards, or never.

The Leadership Action

Legal and Security should record the Anthropic choice before 9 December and update the record of processing activities. For agents, start with an inventory and give each one a human sponsor. Then add agent-targeted Conditional Access in report-only mode, so you see what it would block before it blocks anything. Third-party apps should go through the admin consent workflow in Entra.

The Reality Check

Printing is back on the problem list, where it has always felt at home. The November Windows updates can fail to print documents with invisible zero-size images, such as watermarks or generated templates. They also broke KMS activation scripts until the 13 November out-of-band fix, and October's LDAP hardening can cut access to some NetApp SMB shares. Admins report NTLM signing breaking App Volumes in VDI. On the sign-in side, some SMS and voice MFA sign-ins now show a CAPTCHA, and cross-tenant calendars with partners break unless they're moved off EWS.

The Leadership Action

Keep the security update deployed. This week, ask the template owners in Finance, Legal and HR to test the documents they really need to print. NTLM signing becomes mandatory in May 2027, and starting the audit now is cheaper than starting it in April. The helpdesk needs scripts for the MFA CAPTCHA and the Copilot Chat limits. After 23 November, move cross-tenant calendar sharing together with the partner admins.

Culture, Adoption & Change Velocity

Adoption, habits and trust
Pillar 04 / 04
This week

Demand shows up in the request queue

Sources
First Page Sage ↗Partner Center, Nov 2026 ↗MC1546354 ↗DNSFilter ↗
The Reality Check

Assigned licences say little about adoption, as the idle-seat estimate in Pillar 1 shows. Meanwhile, Copilot Chat limit prompts and the new 'Request access' buttons will create a steady stream of requests from staff. Picture Adele Vance in Finance hitting the Chat limit, clicking 'Request access' and hearing nothing back. If IT blocks requests without an approved alternative, people like Adele move to unapproved tools.

The Leadership Action

Treat licence and access requests as your best demand signal. Send them through one approval path that asks which business process the seat will speed up. Point 'Learn more' to internal guidance with examples from your champions, and let HR own and publish an acceptable-use policy that names the approved tools. When you report on adoption, report time saved in specific processes.

03
Weekly Executive Action List

The 3 calls to make.

01 / 03
Call with CISO / Identity Lead

Agree the Anthropic setting before 9 December. Agent identities need sponsors and their own Conditional Access, and the SMS and voice MFA path needs a decision before 1 February 2027.

02 / 03
Call with Finance / Procurement Lead

Set Copilot spending limits below the credit cap before the next Copilot Business order. That's the urgent one. Plan the 1 December SKU change together with the renewal seat audit, and if SMS MFA stays, budget a telephony provider.

03 / 03
Call with DEX / Endpoint Lead

Start with printing. Can Finance, Legal and HR still print their critical templates on the November build? Then check that the out-of-band fix is in the images and that the NTLM signing audit has started. The helpdesk will need scripts for the MFA CAPTCHA and the Copilot Chat limits.

04
The Anti-Pattern · What to stop doing this week
Stop letting default settings and upgrade prompts make your AI decisions.

This quarter, Microsoft's defaults decide who gets Claude, who sees metered agents and when consumption billing starts. Meanwhile, Chat limit prompts push staff to ask for seats. If you buy seats whenever a prompt asks, or leave settings untouched because nobody owns them, you get shelfware and unbudgeted consumption at the same time, which is quite an achievement. Every date on the cliff list below needs an owner and a recorded choice.
