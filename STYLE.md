# Editorial guide

Every weekly edition of the Workplace Leader Weekly Agenda follows this guide. Read it before drafting, and check the draft against the last section before you publish.

## Who it's for

Enterprise IT managers and leaders in Switzerland, including FINMA-regulated institutions (banks, insurers, asset managers). They're busy, they know Microsoft 365, and they want to know what needs a decision this week.

Flag the Swiss angle where it's real:

- **FINMA**: outsourcing (Circular 2018/3), operational resilience (Circular 2023/1), new subprocessors, data leaving the tenant, audit trails.
- **nFADP** (revDSG): records of processing activities, cross-border disclosure, new processors, data protection impact assessments.
- **Swiss/EU tenant**: EU Data Boundary, Swiss data residency, features that roll out differently for EU and EFTA tenants.

Don't force it. If an item has no Swiss angle, leave it out rather than inventing one.

## What gets in

Each signal has to pass the "So what?" gate: it changes budget or contracts, it changes risk or governance, or it causes friction for people at work. Routine patches and minor UI changes are discarded and listed under "Discarded this week".

The four pillars are standing sections and come back every week in the same order: Financial & Licensing Realities, Governance & Data Hygiene, Digital Employee Experience, and Culture, Adoption & Change Velocity. Only the week's subtitle, Reality Check, Leadership Action and sources change.

## Voice

- Plain, simple English. The author isn't a native speaker, so write clear, everyday wording, not polished consultant English. Short words beat clever ones.
- British/Swiss spelling: organisation, programme, licence (noun), licensed (verb), prioritise, behaviour.
- Use contractions (it's, don't, won't, you'll). Without them the text sounds stiff.
- Dry humour is fine, used sparingly. Never sarcasm about people, teams or vendors' staff.
- Talk to the reader as a peer: say what changes, what it costs or risks, and what to do.
- Dates as "9 December" or "1 February 2027". Times in Swiss time (CET/CEST).

## People and scenarios

- Never invent personal anecdotes or first-person experiences ("At a client last week…", "I've seen…"). If a first-person line would help, write it as a suggestion in a draft note so the author can confirm or replace it.
- For scenarios, use Adele Vance, the standard fictional persona. Make it clear the scenario is illustrative.

## Facts

- Link every fact: dates, figures, quotes, studies, product behaviour, Message Center IDs.
- Never invent sources, MC IDs, dates or numbers. If something can't be verified, leave it out or leave the field empty.
- Prefer primary sources (Microsoft Learn, Message Center, Partner Center, FINMA, FDPIC). Mark community and vendor surveys as such.
- In the Method section, say what was checked, what couldn't be verified and which links are weak.

## AI patterns to avoid

Check the draft against this list and fix what you find before publishing.

- One-sentence paragraphs used for drama ("This is not a feature. / It is a policy.").
- "Not X, it's Y" pivots, and "The point is not… the point is…".
- Balanced pairs (enable or block, before or after, push or pull) more than once per edition.
- Ending every section on a polished aphorism. One per edition, two at most.
- Lists of three by reflex, and stacked lists of five or six items.
- Repeated words and counts ("three paths", "three calls", "three defaults").
- Too many colons used as setup and payoff.
- Too few contractions.
- Identical paragraph shapes in every section. The pillars share a template; the sentences inside them shouldn't all have the same rhythm.

## Before publishing

1. Every item passed the gate, and every fact has a working link.
2. Nothing invented: no anecdotes, no unverified MC IDs, dates or figures.
3. Swiss angle flagged where it's real (FINMA, nFADP, Swiss/EU tenant).
4. Spelling is British/Swiss and contractions are used.
5. The AI-pattern list above has been checked and fixed.
6. The Method section says what wasn't verified.
