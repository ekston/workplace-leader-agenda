Default-On AI vs. Deliberate Control

Over the next ten weeks Microsoft switches on AI features and AI spending unless someone says no. Claude arrives in Swiss and EU tenants on 9 December. The 'Agentic Sprawl' vs. Centralised Governance Trap

Business units are building their own Copilot Studio agents faster than IT can track them, and each one is a new identity with access to company data. Freeze ad-hoc agent publishing and put every autonomous workflow under a credit budget and explicit Conditional Access, with a governed sandbox for the teams that want to keep building.
